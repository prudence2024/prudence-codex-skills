# Legal Business reasoning and integration

## Fact provenance

For every material statement record its source, owner, observation date, and
status: verified fact, owner decision, assumption, unresolved question, or
counsel-dependent conclusion. Prefer implemented behavior and signed business
decisions over marketing language or intended future controls.

## Cross-document consistency

Maintain a canonical glossary and fact sheet. Compare entity names, defined
terms, roles, effective dates, products, plans, fees, taxes, renewals, refunds,
support, SLAs, RTO/RPO, security commitments, incident notification, data
categories, purposes, lawful bases, vendors, subprocessors, transfers,
retention, deletion, liability, governing law, notice channels, and contacts.

Do not silently resolve conflicts. Record the conflict, affected documents,
decision owner, deadline, and publication blocker.

## Shared Context and handoffs

Read project facts, decisions, artifacts, risks, uncertainty, and prior runs.
Consume verified Security controls, Incident Response capabilities, Support
Triage commitments, and owner decisions. Write attributable legal facts,
document decisions, drafts, risks, uncertainties, approval states, and handoffs.

Security facts do not become compliance conclusions automatically. Incident or
support targets do not become contractual promises until operational and
business owners approve them.

## Review and publication gates

Distinguish drafted, internally reviewed, owner approved, counsel reviewed,
approved for publication, published, accepted, and superseded. Never collapse
these evidence states. External publication, signature, filing, or acceptance
requires explicit authorization.

## Extensions

Jurisdiction guidance, clause libraries, consistency tools, and approval systems
are advisory inputs with source, version, jurisdiction, effective date, and
limitations. Legal Business retains final drafting and uncertainty-reporting
ownership; qualified counsel retains legal approval.
