# Legal & Business Protection

Use these materials as drafting and issue-spotting aids, not final legal advice. Require qualified legal review before publication, signature, or reliance—especially for Nigeria-based operations, Delta State or Lagos clients, regulated data, international customers, or EU/UK users.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the actual product, data flows, vendors, hosting regions, customer types, pricing, refund behavior, support commitments, and target jurisdictions. Never claim a practice the product does not follow.
2. Build a fact sheet and list unresolved business/legal decisions before drafting.
3. Select only the documents the engagement needs, then adapt the starting clauses in [references/document-templates.md](references/document-templates.md).
4. Keep consumer terms separate from negotiated B2B agreements where their obligations differ.
5. Cross-check definitions, entity names, dates, governing law, liability caps, data roles, subprocessors, service levels, refund rules, and contact details across every document.
6. Mark uncertain or jurisdiction-dependent clauses as `[LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED]`; never invent a registration number, address, regulator filing, insurance coverage, or compliance certification.
7. Compare the Privacy Policy and DPA against implemented telemetry, cookies, retention, deletion, backups, payment handling, and vendor transfers.
8. Obtain business-owner approval and qualified legal review before documents go live or are signed.

## Required launch set

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Data Processing Agreement for B2B customers
- Refund Policy
- Master Service Agreement
- Cyber-liability insurance readiness checklist

## Guardrails

- Do not guarantee GDPR, Nigeria Data Protection Act/NDPR, consumer-protection, tax, employment, sectoral, or cross-border compliance from a template alone.
- Do not promise uptime, response times, refunds, indemnities, or insurance controls the business cannot operationally meet.
- Use plain language for customer-facing documents while retaining precise defined terms.
- Date and version every published policy and retain prior versions plus acceptance evidence where required.
