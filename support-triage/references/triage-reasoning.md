# Support Triage reasoning and integration

## Classification evidence

Record the customer request, confirmed impact, relevant timeline, identifiers,
environment, current known incidents, known-path match, authorization, actions
already taken, ruled-out causes, and uncertainty. Use the highest-risk applicable
tier; never lower a tier merely to improve automation rate.

## Known-path requirements

A resolution path must have an owner, version or review date, preconditions,
authorized actions, prohibited actions, expected result, verification method,
failure handling, rollback when applicable, customer wording, and closure
criteria. Missing or stale requirements force assisted triage or human review.

## Shared Context and privacy

Read relevant project facts, decisions, incidents, risks, artifacts, and prior
runs. Write minimized support facts, classifications, decisions, outcome
evidence, uncertainties, and handoffs. Link to restricted evidence rather than
copying secrets, full payment data, tokens, sensitive payloads, or unnecessary
personal information.

## Specialist handoffs

- Incident Response receives suspected or confirmed service incidents and
  customer-impact evidence; Support consumes approved status language.
- Security receives abuse, security, privacy, identity, or suspicious-access
  reports.
- Legal Business or the authorized business owner receives refunds, billing
  disputes, policy exceptions, legal requests, compensation, or commitments.
- Product and engineering receive complete reproducible issues and feature
  requests without Support deciding roadmap or implementation.

## Reporting states

Distinguish classified, action attempted, action succeeded, response sent,
response received, owner accepted, customer resolved, verified, and closed.
State checks not run and why.
